How to play Compare Clash

  1. Read the challenge card in the middle, e.g. BIGGER?, CHEAPER? or Closest in size to a bus.
  2. Pick your best word card from your hand of 6. Think about the real thing: is a barn bigger than a car?
  3. Add one action card if you like (Boost, Shrink, Reverse…).
  4. Gambit: bet Vocab Points that you will WIN (pays 1:1) or LOSE (pays 2:1). Great for a weak hand!
  5. Cards flip, actions happen, and the winner gets 5 VP. Read the sentence: “A barn is bigger than a car.”

Discards: 3 free per game — swap up to 2 cards each time. After that it costs 3 VP.

Some words can’t play! A house can’t move, and the sun can’t be bought. Those cards are out of the round.

Everyone starts with 20 VP. The player with the most VP after the last round wins. In Free Play, the host finishes the game whenever they like.
